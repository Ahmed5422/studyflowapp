## 🌟 What Makes StudyFlow Different?

Ordinary PDF viewers were built for office memos. **StudyFlow was built specifically for students studying complex slide decks, medicine, engineering, and law.**

### 1. 🎯 Zero-Waste Presentation Mode
- Eliminates letterboxing, black side gaps, and wasted borders.
- Slides dynamically scale edge-to-edge to fill 100% of your screen width and height.
- Focus mode (`F11`) gives you distraction-free studying with an auto-hiding study bar.

### 2. 🧠 Deep AI Quiz Customizer
- **Total Freedom in Question Counts**: Choose anywhere from 1 to 100 questions via smooth slider or direct numeric input.
- **Selective Slide Coverage**: Test yourself across the entire document, just the active slide, or a custom range (e.g. Slides 10 to 35).
- **Multiple Formats**: Toggle MCQs (4 choices), True/False, Clinical/Technical Case Vignettes, and High-Yield Distractor Traps.
- **Timed Exam Pacing**: Practice mode with instant rationales vs realistic timed board exam countdowns (e.g. 1 min/q or custom minutes).
- **NotebookLM-Style Guidance**: Inject custom focus notes or click instant high-yield chips (*Pathophysiology, Pharmacology, Formulas, Differential Diagnoses*).

### 3. 💾 PowerPoint-Style Realtime AutoSave
- Persistent real-time state tracking modeled after Microsoft 365 AutoSave.
- Every highlight, active note, reading coordinate, and open tab is saved instantly to local storage.
- Unexpected power outages or closing the app will never lose your place.
- Accidental exit guard prevents accidental closure with a confirmation prompt.

### 4. 🎴 Spaced Repetition & Flashcard Synthesizer
- Instantly transform complex lecture slides into interactive flip flashcards.
- SuperMemo SM-2 spaced repetition engine (`Again`, `Hard`, `Good`, `Easy`) schedules cards at optimal memory intervals.

### 5. 🪟 Windows 11 Native Snap & Glass Integration
- Frameless dark cinema window with native Windows 11 Snap Layout support.
- Starts maximized by default on launch for immediate productivity.
- Isolated Study Tools Suite drawer positioned cleanly below window caption controls.
